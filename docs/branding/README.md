# TAClient icon

An original vector play mark inside an outlined video frame, with one archive shelf beneath it. The vermilion and play motif acknowledge the video source; the outlined frame and shelf give TAClient its own archive identity. This is not the YouTube logo and implies no affiliation.

`swift scripts/generate-app-icon.swift` regenerates the three SVG sources here and the corresponding opaque 1024×1024 PNGs in `AppIcon.appiconset`. Default, dark and monochrome tinted variants have separate masters. The production assets have square, full-bleed backgrounds; iOS supplies the app-icon mask. No text, tiny details or baked corner mask is used.
